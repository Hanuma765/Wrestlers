IYO SKY

IYO SKY is one of WWE's most exciting and athletic superstars. Known for her incredible high-flying ability, fearless attitude, and technical wrestling skills, she has become one of the biggest stars in women's wrestling.

ABOUT IYO SKY

IYO SKY is a Japanese professional wrestler known for her spectacular aerial offense and unique wrestling style. Before joining WWE, she became a major star in Japan.

In WWE, IYO has competed in NXT and on the main roster, becoming a major part of several championship matches and women's factions.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • Former WWE Women's Champion
  • Former WWE Women's Tag Team Champion
  • Former NXT Women's Champion
  • Former WWE Women's Money in the Bank winner
  • Former NXT Women's Tag Team Champion
  • Winner of the 2023 Women's Money in the Bank Ladder Match
  • Former World Tag Team Champion in Japan
  • Multiple-time champion throughout her wrestling career

DAMAGE CTRL

IYO SKY was one of the founding members of Damage CTRL alongside Bayley and Dakota Kai. The group became one of the most important women's factions in WWE.

IYO and Dakota Kai also formed a successful tag team, while Bayley served as the group's leader.

IYO SKY has shared a strong partnership with Asuka and Kairi Sane. Asuka and Kairi are known as The Kabuki Warriors, while IYO has competed alongside both wrestlers in different situations.

IYO's history with Asuka and Kairi connects her WWE career with some of Japan's most respected women's wrestlers.

WRESTLING STYLE

IYO SKY combines high-flying moves with technical wrestling and hard-hitting strikes. Her athleticism allows her to perform moves from the top rope and around the ring that many wrestlers cannot.

Her moonsault is one of her most recognizable moves and is a major part of her identity as "The Genius of the Sky."

THE GENIUS OF THE SKY

The nickname "The Genius of the Sky" represents IYO's creativity, athleticism, and ability to attack from unexpected angles.

Her combination of speed, balance, aerial ability, and confidence makes her stand out whenever she steps into the ring.

MEMORABLE MOMENTS

  • Winning the 2023 Women's Money in the Bank Ladder Match
  • Cashing in Money in the Bank to become WWE Women's Champion
  • Competing at WrestleMania
  • Becoming NXT Women's Champion
  • Being a founding member of Damage CTRL
  • Competing in major WWE women's championship matches

WHY I LIKE IYO SKY

I like IYO SKY because she is incredibly athletic and exciting to watch. Her high-flying wrestling style makes her matches different from many other WWE matches.

I also like how fearless she is in the ring. Her personality, entrance, wrestling ability, and spectacular moonsault make her one of my favourite WWE superstars.

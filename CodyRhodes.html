CODY RHODES

The American Nightmare

THE AMERICAN NIGHTMARE

Cody Rhodes is one of WWE's biggest stars and is known as "The American Nightmare." His career has taken him from WWE to the independent wrestling scene and back to WWE, where he has competed for some of the biggest championships in professional wrestling.

ABOUT CODY RHODES

Cody Rhodes is a second-generation professional wrestler and the son of WWE Hall of Famer Dusty Rhodes. He first became well known in WWE before leaving the company and building a major career outside WWE.

Cody eventually returned to WWE and became known as "The American Nightmare." His story of trying to finish what his family started has become a major part of his WWE career.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • WWE Champion
  • Multiple-time Intercontinental Champion
  • Multiple-time WWE Tag Team Champion
  • WWE Royal Rumble winner
  • Two-time Royal Rumble winner
  • Winner of the 2023 Men's Royal Rumble Match
  • Winner of the 2024 Men's Royal Rumble Match
  • AEW TNT Champion
  • AEW World Tag Team Champion
  • Co-founder of All Elite Wrestling

THE AMERICAN NIGHTMALE

Cody adopted the nickname "The American Nightmare" during his time outside WWE. The name became a major part of his identity and followed him when he returned to WWE.

His entrance, American-themed presentation, and championship ambitions have all become important parts of his character.

THE RHODES FAMILY

Wrestling has always been a huge part of Cody Rhodes' life. His father, Dusty Rhodes, was one of the most influential wrestlers in professional wrestling history.

Cody's brother Dustin Rhodes, better known to WWE fans as Goldust, is also a professional wrestler. Cody has teamed with his brother during several important moments in his career.

WRESTLEMANIA

WrestleMania has played a major role in Cody Rhodes' story. His championship journey eventually led to major WrestleMania matches against Roman Reigns.

Cody's WrestleMania matches have become some of the most important moments of his WWE career.

FINISH THE STORY

"Finish the Story" became one of Cody Rhodes' most famous phrases. It refers to his goal of winning the WWE Championship and completing a journey that had personal meaning because of his family's history in wrestling.

WRESTLING STYLE

Cody combines technical wrestling, powerful strikes, athletic moves, and traditional professional wrestling storytelling. He also uses his background and family history to make his matches feel personal.

WHY I LIKE CODY RHODES

I like Cody Rhodes because his career has a really interesting story. He left WWE, became successful outside the company, and eventually returned to WWE as one of its biggest stars.

I also like his entrance, his American-themed presentation, his wrestling style, and the story behind "The American Nightmare."

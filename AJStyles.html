AJ STYLES

AJ Styles, known as "The Phenomenal One", is one of the most recognizable wrestlers of his generation. He is known for his athletic ability, technical wrestling, high-flying moves and incredible matches.

AJ Styles made his WWE debut in the 2016 Royal Rumble Match and went on to become a WWE Champion, United States Champion, Intercontinental Champion and Tag Team Champion.

ABOUT AJ STYLES

AJ Styles is a professional wrestler from Gainesville, Georgia. Before joining WWE, Styles became famous around the world through his work in promotions such as TNA and New Japan Pro-Wrestling.

He became known as "The Phenomenal One" because of his combination of athleticism, technical wrestling, striking ability and spectacular aerial moves.

Styles officially arrived in WWE at the 2016 Royal Rumble, entering at No. 3 and immediately becoming one of the biggest names in WWE.

WHO HAS AJ STYLES TEAMED WITH?

AJ Styles has teamed with several different WWE Superstars throughout his career.

AJ Styles & Omos

AJ Styles formed a tag team with Omos in 2021. The team defeated The New Day at WrestleMania 37 to become Raw Tag Team Champions.

AJ Styles & Karl Anderson & Luke Gallows

Styles reunited with Karl Anderson and Luke Gallows to form The O.C. The group became one of Styles' most recognizable alliances in WWE.

AJ Styles & Chris Jericho

Styles and Chris Jericho briefly teamed together as Y2AJ. They attempted to win the WWE Tag Team Championship before their partnership ended.

AJ Styles & Dragon Lee

Styles also teamed with Dragon Lee. The two formed an exciting high-flying combination and competed together for the World Tag Team Championship.

THE O.C.

The O.C. is one of AJ Styles' most important WWE alliances. The group included AJ Styles, Luke Gallows and Karl Anderson.

The group originally reunited in 2019 and later returned together in 2022. Michin was also associated with the group during its later WWE run.

Styles had already known Anderson and Gallows from their time wrestling in Japan.

  • Spiral Tap

SIGNATURE MOVES

Phenomenal Forearm

AJ Styles launches himself from the ropes and strikes his opponent with a flying forearm.

Styles Clash

One of AJ's most famous finishing moves. Styles lifts his opponent onto his shoulders and drives them down to the mat.

Calf Crusher

A submission move where Styles attacks his opponent's leg using a leg-locking hold.

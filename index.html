Wrestlers I Admire

  • Jimmy Uso

    Part of The Usos, who are 9x tag team champions and one of the most successful tag teams in WWE history. Jimmy was also part of The Bloodline alongside Roman Reigns, Solo Sikoa, Sami Zayn, Jey Uso and Paul Heyman.

  • Jey Uso

    Part of The Usos, who are 9x tag team champions and one of the most successful tag teams in WWE history. Jey was also part of The Bloodline alongside Roman Reigns, Solo Sikoa, Sami Zayn, Jimmy Uso and Paul Heyman.

  • Penta

    Known for "Cero Miedo". Penta was part of the Lucha Bros with Rey Fenix and became a former Intercontinental Champion. He has had great matches and defenses against wrestlers such as Je'Von Evans and Dragon Lee.

  • Rey Fenix

    Part of the Lucha Bros with Penta. Rey Fenix is known for his incredible athleticism, high-flying style and exciting matches.

  • Kairi Sane

    Part of The Kabuki Warriors with Asuka. Kairi is known for her high-flying wrestling style, great character work and memorable matches.

  • IYO Sky

    Women's World Champion and former undisputed women's champion. IYO has had great matches against Kairi Sane, Lyra Valkyria, Bayley, Giulia, Rhea Ripley, Asuka and many more.

  • Lyra Valkyria

    First ever Women's Intercontinental Champion. Lyra is known for her technical wrestling, athletic ability and competitive matches.

  • Dragon Lee

    Known for his extreme speed, tornado-style suicide dives and dives over the announce table. He has had exciting matches against wrestlers such as Penta, Rey Fenix and Will Ospreay.

  • Finn Balor

    Former member of The Judgment Day and the first ever Universal Champion. Finn has competed in some huge matches throughout his WWE career and is known for his Demon character as well as his normal wrestling persona.

  • Giulia

    Former United States Champion. Giulia is known for her hard-hitting wrestling style, confidence and strong character work.

DRAGON LEE

The High-Flying Superstar

DRAGON LEE

Dragon Lee is known for his incredible speed, high-flying attacks and exciting wrestling style.

His matches are filled with fast-paced action, spectacular dives and incredible athletic ability.

ABOUT DRAGON LEE

Dragon Lee is a professional wrestler known for his fast-paced lucha libre style and high-risk moves. He has competed against some of the biggest names in professional wrestling.

One of the things that makes Dragon Lee stand out is his incredible speed and his ability to perform spectacular dives outside of the ring.

WRESTLING STYLE

  • High-flying wrestling
  • Extremely fast-paced offense
  • Tornado suicide dives
  • Dives over the announce table
  • Lucha libre influence
  • Technical wrestling

NOTABLE OPPONENTS

  • Penta
  • Rey Fenix
  • Will Ospreay
  • Dominik Mysterio
  • Oba Femi
  • Wes Lee
  • Finn Balor
  • JD McDonaugh
  • Legado del Fantasma (Santos Escobar, Angel Garza, Humberto Carrilio)

WHY I LIKE DRAGON LEE

Dragon Lee is one of my favourite wrestlers because of his extreme speed and his incredible aerial moves.

His tornado suicide dives and dives over the announce table make his matches especially exciting to watch.

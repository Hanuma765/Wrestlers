JEY USO

Main Event Jey Uso

MAIN EVENT JEY USO

Jey Uso is a professional wrestler known for his incredible tag team career with his twin brother Jimmy Uso and his rise as a major singles Superstar.

Jey became known as "Main Event Jey Uso" after becoming one of WWE's most popular and recognizable Superstars. His energetic entrances, Uso Splash and YEET movement have made him a huge fan favourite.

ABOUT JEY USO

Jey Uso is one half of The Usos alongside his twin brother, Jimmy Uso. Together, they became one of the most successful tag teams in WWE history.

Jey later developed into a major singles Superstar. His journey from being part of The Bloodline to becoming "Main Event" Jey Uso became one of the biggest stories of his WWE career.

Jey is known for his energetic personality, his "YEET" catchphrase, his Uso Splash and his connection with the WWE Universe.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • 9x WWE Tag Team Champion
  • WWE SmackDown Tag Team Champion
  • WWE Raw Tag Team Champion
  • Undisputed WWE Tag Team Champion
  • Royal Rumble winner
  • WWE Intercontinental Champion (defeated Bron Breakker to win the title)
  • WWE World Heavyweight Champion (defeated Gunther at Wrestlemania to win the title)
  • Multiple-time WrestleMania competitor

Jey's greatest tag team achievement came alongside Jimmy Uso, when The Usos became one of the longest-reigning tag teams and most successful tag team in WWE history.

THE USOS

Jey Uso and Jimmy Uso are twin brothers who formed The Usos. The team became one of WWE's most successful tag teams.

Members

  • Jey Uso
  • Jimmy Uso

The Usos became famous for their Uso Splash, superkicks, fast-paced tag team wrestling and incredible chemistry.

They competed against many of WWE's greatest tag teams, including The New Day, The Shield, The Street Profits and The Revival.

THE BLOODLINE

Jey Uso became a major part of The Bloodline alongside Roman Reigns, Jimmy Uso, Solo Sikoa and Paul Heyman.

Important Members

  • Roman Reigns
  • Jey Uso
  • Jimmy Uso
  • Solo Sikoa
  • Paul Heyman
  • Sami Zayn (honourary uce)

Jey's relationship with Roman Reigns became one of the most important parts of his WWE career. Their storyline helped establish Jey as a major singles Superstar.

Jey eventually separated himself from The Bloodline and began establishing his own identity outside of Roman Reigns' shadow. He came to RAW to team with Cody Rhodes, Sami Zayn, Kevin Owens vs Judgment Day

SIGNATURE MOVES

Uso Splash

Jey's famous top-rope splash is one of his most recognizable finishing moves.

Superkick

Spears

Jey has also used the spear as a powerful attack during his singles career.

Running Hip Attack

Jey can use his speed to hit opponents with a running hip attack in the corner.

MAJOR RIVALS

Roman Reigns

Roman Reigns is one of the most important rivals in Jey's career. Their rivalry began during the Bloodline storyline and helped establish Jey as a singles Superstar.

Sami Zayn

Jey and Sami Zayn developed a complicated relationship during The Bloodline storyline.

Jimmy Uso

Jey has also had major conflicts with his own twin brother, Jimmy Uso. Their brother-versus-brother matches became an important part of their story.

Gunther

Jey has competed against Gunther in major singles matches, including matches involving championship opportunities.

Solo Sikoa

Jey has also had several conflicts with Solo Sikoa after the Bloodline began changing.

MAIN EVENT JEY USO

Jey's transformation from one half of The Usos into a singles Superstar became a major part of his character.

Instead of being known only as Jimmy Uso's brother or Roman Reigns' cousin, Jey began creating his own identity.

His "Main Event" nickname represents his growth into a singles Superstar.

WHY I LIKE JEY USO

I like Jey Uso because he has had one of the most interesting journeys in WWE. He went from being one half of The Usos to becoming a major singles Superstar.

I really like his connection with Jimmy Uso and the history they have together as The Usos.

I also like Jey's Bloodline storyline because it showed a different side of his character and helped him become "Main Event" Jey Uso.

His entrance, energy, YEET catchphrase and connection with the crowd are also some of my favourite things about him.

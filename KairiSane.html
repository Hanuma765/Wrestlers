KAIRI SANE

The Pirate Princess

THE PIRATE PRINCESS

Kairi Sane is a Japanese professional wrestler known as "The Pirate Princess." She is famous for her incredible elbow drop, high-flying wrestling style, athleticism and pirate-themed character.

Kairi became a major star in WWE after winning the inaugural Mae Young Classic in 2017 and later becoming NXT Women's Champion.

ABOUT KAIRI SANE

Kairi Sane was born in Yamaguchi, Japan and became known around the world for her wrestling ability and unique personality.

Before becoming a WWE Superstar, Kairi competed in Japan and became one of the most successful wrestlers of her generation.

In WWE, Kairi became known as The Pirate Princess. Her entrance, pirate-inspired gear and famous InSane Elbow helped make her one of the most recognizable wrestlers in the women's division.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • 2017 Mae Young Classic winner
  • NXT Women's Champion
  • WWE Women's Tag Team Champion
  • IWGP Women's Champion
  • Multiple-time tag team champion in Japan
  • Former WWE Women's Tag Team Champion with Asuka

One of Kairi's biggest WWE achievements was winning the inaugural Mae Young Classic in 2017. She later defeated Shayna Baszler at NXT TakeOver: Brooklyn IV to become NXT Women's Champion.

THE KABUKI WARRIORS

Kairi Sane and Asuka formed one of the most recognizable women's tag teams in WWE history: The Kabuki Warriors.

The team combined Kairi's high-flying wrestling with Asuka's aggressive striking and submission style.

Members

  • Kairi Sane
  • Asuka

Together, Kairi and Asuka captured the WWE Women's Tag Team Championship multiple times.

DAMAGE CTRL

THE SKY PIRATES

Kairi Sane and IYO SKY were known as the Sky Pirates during their time together in NXT.

The two had a very exciting combination of high-flying offense and athletic moves.

Kairi and IYO also became close friends outside the ring, making their partnership especially memorable.

SIGNATURE MOVES

InSane Elbow

Kairi's most famous move is the InSane Elbow. She climbs to the top rope and delivers a devastating elbow drop onto her opponent.

Anchor

The Anchor is another signature move associated with Kairi Sane.

Sliding D

Kairi has also used striking attacks and running moves to surprise her opponents.

Interceptor

Kairi can use her speed and timing to hit opponents with quick attacks before they can recover.

MAJOR RIVALS

Shayna Baszler

Shayna Baszler is one of Kairi's most important rivals. Their rivalry began in the 2017 Mae Young Classic Final, where Kairi defeated Baszler.

Their rivalry continued in NXT, eventually leading to Kairi winning the NXT Women's Championship from Baszler.

Bianca Belair

Kairi and Bianca Belair had several memorable encounters during Kairi's NXT run.

Becky Lynch

Kairi has also faced Becky Lynch in WWE, including during the period when Kairi and Asuka were WWE Women's Tag Team Champions.

IYO SKY

IYO SKY was once one of Kairi's closest allies. Their relationship became more complicated as their WWE storylines changed.

Rhea Ripley

Rhea Ripley became involved in the storyline surrounding IYO SKY, Asuka and Kairi, leading to major matches and conflicts between the two sides.

WRESTLING STYLE

Kairi's wrestling style combines speed, athleticism, technical wrestling and high-flying attacks.

  • High-flying attacks
  • Elbow strikes
  • Top-rope offense
  • Quick counters
  • Submission wrestling
  • Strong striking

Her most recognizable move is the InSane Elbow, which she performs from the top rope.

THE PIRATE PRINCESS

Kairi's pirate character is one of the most unique presentations in WWE.

Her entrance, pirate-inspired gear, ship's wheel imagery and "Pirate Princess" nickname all help create her distinctive character.

WHY I LIKE KAIRI SANE

I like Kairi Sane because she has a really unique character and wrestling style. The Pirate Princess gimmick makes her stand out from everyone else.

I also love her matches because she combines high-flying moves with hard-hitting attacks. Her InSane Elbow is one of my favourite finishing moves.

Another reason I like Kairi is her teamwork with Asuka. The Kabuki Warriors are one of my favourite women's tag teams, and I really enjoy watching their chemistry together.

I also like her friendship and history with IYO SKY, which makes their matches and storylines even more interesting.

SOL RUCA

Sol Ruca is one of WWE's most exciting young Superstars. She is known for her incredible athleticism, speed, flexibility and unique high-flying wrestling style.

Sol brings an energetic and athletic style to the ring, combining wrestling with the skills she developed as an athlete and surfer.

ABOUT SOL RUCA

Sol Ruca is a professional wrestler who competes in WWE. She became known for her incredible athletic ability and spectacular moves.

Before becoming a wrestler, Sol had experience in gymnastics and surfing. These athletic backgrounds helped shape her unique wrestling style.

Sol's combination of speed, flexibility and creativity has made her one of the most exciting young talents in WWE.

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • WWE NXT Superstar
  • NXT Women's North American Championship contender
  • Competed in NXT Women's Breakout Tournament
  • Competed in major NXT women's matches
  • Known for spectacular high-flying performances

Sol has quickly established herself as one of the most exciting competitors in the NXT women's division.

WRESTLING STYLE

Sol Ruca's wrestling style is based around athleticism, speed, flexibility and high-flying offense.

  • High-flying wrestling
  • Springboard attacks
  • Flips and aerial moves
  • Quick counters
  • Acrobatic offense
  • Agility

Her athletic background allows her to perform moves that require an incredible amount of flexibility and body control.

SIGNATURE MOVES

Sol Snatcher

The Sol Snatcher is Sol Ruca's spectacular finishing move. She uses her flexibility and athleticism to launch herself into an incredible flipping attack.

Springboard Moves

Sol frequently uses the ropes to launch herself at her opponents with fast and unpredictable attacks.

High-Flying Attacks

Sol is known for using flips, dives and other aerial attacks to take down her opponents.

THE WAVE

Sol Ruca's presentation has a strong connection to the ocean, surfing and beach culture.

Her name, entrance and personality help create an energetic surfer-inspired character that separates her from other WWE Superstars.

ATHLETIC BACKGROUND

Sol's athletic background plays a major role in her wrestling style.

  • Gymnastics
  • Surfing
  • Acrobatics
  • Strength and conditioning

These skills help explain why Sol is able to perform extremely difficult aerial and acrobatic moves inside the wrestling ring.
